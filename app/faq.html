Frequently Asked Questions

What Aurora Forge does, what it does not do, and how to use a finished handoff pack.

The short answer

Aurora Forge builds the instructions

Describe or select the WWE 2K26 result you want. Aurora Forge turns those choices into a structured prompt and, where needed, packages technical profiles, references, checklists, and project notes. You decide which compatible AI or image tool will create the result.

Choose a Prompt Builder

What is Aurora Forge?

Aurora Forge is a WWE 2K26 prompt-building and workflow-preparation workspace. Its guided builders help users create technically detailed prompts for faces, masks, tattoos, attire, logos, belts, arenas, complete characters, and other supported creative work.

Is Aurora Forge an AI chatbot?

No. Aurora Forge is not a conversational assistant and does not answer a prompt as a chatbot would. It prepares the instructions and supporting files that the user takes to a compatible AI or image-generation tool.

Does Aurora Forge generate the finished artwork?

No. Aurora Forge generates the prompt and handoff materials. The external tool chosen by the user generates the image or other creative result.

What does Aurora Forge actually generate?

Depending on the selected builder, Aurora Forge can produce a finished prompt, project brief, checklist, profile JSON, layout references, expected-output notes, and a ZIP handoff pack containing the files needed for the next step.

Why not type a short request directly into an AI tool?

WWE 2K26 texture work has technical constraints that are easy to omit. Aurora Forge helps specify the target, layout, dimensions, materials, required files, preservation rules, things to avoid, and validation steps so the external tool receives a clearer request.

Do I need to understand prompt engineering?

No. The builders use normal project questions and domain-specific choices. Aurora Forge assembles those choices into detailed instructions automatically.

What is a handoff pack?

A handoff pack is a ZIP containing the finished prompt and the supporting technical files required by that workflow. Review its contents, then upload the relevant files to the compatible AI or image tool you choose.

Is Aurora Forge tied to ChatGPT or one AI provider?

No. Aurora Forge does not send prompts automatically and is not tied to a single provider. Compatibility depends on whether the chosen tool can accept the prompt and required reference files.

Can I review, copy, or export the prompt?

Yes. Builders provide prompt or handoff exports, and applicable workflows provide copy or download controls. Review the instructions and project choices before using them externally.

What does the repackager do?

It gathers an extracted mod project into one portable ZIP, adds a file-and-checksum manifest, and verifies the completed package. It does not edit or replace the game’s original CAK archives.

Does Aurora Forge send my project to an AI automatically?

No. Aurora Forge prepares files locally. The user controls if, when, and where a prompt or handoff pack is uploaded.

What are the other tools for?

Project, inspection, preview, conversion, setup, and tutorial tools support the prompt-to-game workflow. They organize or validate the work; they do not turn Aurora Forge into a chatbot and do not replace the external creative-generation step.

Who is Aurora Forge for?

It is for WWE 2K26 creators who want clearer prompts, repeatable project organization, technically appropriate supporting files, and a guided path from an idea to a testable in-game result.

Is Aurora Forge open source?

Yes. Aurora Forge and its extraction-helper source are available under the MIT License. Required third-party license notices are included with the project.
